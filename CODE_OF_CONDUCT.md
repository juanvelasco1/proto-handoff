# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or the language they
write in. Issues and discussions may be in English or Spanish.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Help newcomers; nobody starts knowing how Figma, MCP or agent skills work.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's prototype, client data, Figma links or personal
  information.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block participants who
don't follow this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment
or issue, or contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.
